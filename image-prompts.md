# The Badminton Playbook: image prompts

One prompt per Learning, for Higgsfield or any image generator. Shared style so the 8 images read as one set.

**Shared style (add to every prompt):** editorial illustration, flat shapes with fine line work, palette of deep court green (#164B3F), off-white (#F4F6F3) and a single shuttlecock-cork amber accent (#E3A21E), indoor badminton hall light, generous empty space, no text, no logos, landscape 16:10.

1. **Onboarding & The Art of Patience.** A beginner on an indoor badminton court mid-swing, completely missing a shuttlecock that floats past the racket, the service line under their feet; in the background an experienced player calmly demonstrating a correct grip.
2. **Radical Inclusivity & Meritocracy.** A top-down view of a badminton court where very different people (an executive in a polo, a college student, an older player) dive side by side to keep a rally alive; the court boundary lines glow softly.
3. **Upgrading Your Tech Stack (The Gear).** A close-up still life of two rackets side by side: a heavy generic frame and a finely engineered racket showing string tension, a sweat-absorbent grip and a marked balance point.
4. **The Power of the Partner (Team Motivation).** Two doubles partners tapping rackets together after a missed point, one reassuring the other, the net and opponents softly out of focus.
5. **Anticipation over Reaction.** A calm professional player already standing where the shuttlecock will land, with a dotted trajectory arc showing its future path; a blurred beginner lunging late in the background.
6. **Corporate Mentorship (The Seniors).** A senior player in the centre of the court handing a bottle of water to a younger player after a match, pointing toward the corners of the court as if explaining placement.
7. **Preparation & Debriefs (Warm-ups & Cool-downs).** Split scene: on the left, players stretching and warming up before a match; on the right, the same players cooling down and talking it over after the match.
8. **Bouncing Back from Injuries & Resilience.** A player with a taped knee lacing their shoes on the court sideline, ready to step back in, a racket and shuttlecock resting beside them in warm light.
